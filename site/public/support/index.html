Reporting a problem

To help us find the cause quickly, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (iOS Settings → General → About).
  • Your Harbor version (in Harbor, tap … → Settings, then look under About Harbor).
  • The website involved, if any, and the steps that lead to the problem.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps show what happened.

Harbor keeps your browsing on your device, so we can’t see your history or tabs. Please don’t send passwords or browsing information you’d prefer to keep private. Support emails are deleted every 30 days, as described in our privacy policy.

If you’re testing Harbor through TestFlight, you can also send feedback and screenshots from the TestFlight app.

Making Harbor your default browser

In Harbor, tap … → Settings → Default browser. This opens iOS Settings, where you can choose Harbor. You can also go to iOS Settings → Apps → Default Apps → Browser App and select Harbor. This setting applies to all profiles.

Tracker protection

A website isn’t working properly

Some videos and sign-in pages don’t work while trackers are blocked. On that website, tap … → Site protection, turn off Block trackers on this website, then tap Reload to apply to this page. The exception is saved for that exact hostname.

Managing website exceptions

Go to Settings → Privacy → Website exceptions. To turn protection back on for a site, swipe left on it and tap Remove.

Turning protections on or off

Tracker blocking and Remove tracking parameters are in Settings → Privacy. Both are on by default and apply to the current profile. Harbor helps reduce tracking, but it isn’t a VPN and doesn’t make browsing anonymous.

Changing your search engine

Go to Settings → Search → Search engine and choose DuckDuckGo, Google, or Bing.

Profiles

Each profile has its own tabs, history, bookmarks, website sign-ins, and browsing settings.

Switching profiles

Tap the profile icon at the left of the address bar, then choose a profile. Switching profiles reloads pages and stops any media that’s playing.

Creating a profile

Go to Settings → Profiles → New profile, then choose a name and an icon.

Locking a profile with Face ID or Touch ID

When you create or edit a profile, turn on Require Face ID (or Touch ID, or device authentication). Use Lock after to choose how long Harbor waits to lock after you leave the app. Anyone who knows your device passcode can unlock the profile.

Deleting a profile

In Settings → Profiles, swipe left on the profile and tap Delete. This permanently removes the profile’s tabs, history, bookmarks, settings, and website data. You must have at least one profile.

Clearing history and website data

History

Tap … → History. Swipe left on a visit to delete it, tap Select to remove several, or tap Clear All. Clearing history doesn’t sign you out of websites.

One website

On that website, tap … → Site protection → Clear website data. You may be signed out of the site and of related subdomains. Reload the page when you’re ready to reconnect.

Everything in a profile

Go to Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data. This closes the profile’s tabs and deletes its history and website data, which signs you out of websites. Bookmarks, settings, and website exceptions are kept.

The “Not secure” warning

An orange warning triangle in the address bar means the page was loaded over an unencrypted HTTP connection. Others on the same network may be able to see or change what you send and receive. Avoid entering passwords or personal details on these pages. If the site supports it, try typing its address with https:// at the start.

Downloads

When a download finishes, Harbor opens the share sheet so you can save the file to the Files app or send it to another app. If the share sheet doesn’t appear, tap … → Downloads. Harbor stores downloads only temporarily and removes them when the app restarts, so save files you want to keep.
